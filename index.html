GOOD TO KNOW

Before your
appointment.

Do I need an appointment?

Advance booking is recommended to secure your preferred time, although walk-ins may be accepted when capacity allows.

Can I get a consultation first?

Yes. Bada Hair publishes a free consultation option from Monday to Thursday. The team can assess your hair or scalp and suggest suitable services.

What payments are accepted?

The official FAQ lists cash, Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, NETS, PayNow, UnionPay, GrabPay and contactless payments.

What if I am late?

The salon asks guests to inform the team as soon as possible. Appointments more than 15 minutes late may need to be adjusted or cancelled depending on availability.
